Iva Tewari is a Product Designer based in Delhi, currently working at i3 Digital Health. Her work spans user research, journey mapping, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping.

She holds a Bachelor of Arts from Daulat Ram College, University of Delhi (Dept. Rank 1, CGPA 8.19/10).
